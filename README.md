# UK Home Decarbonisation Advisor

Interactive screening tool for a UK home: running-cost change, net cost after grants and simple payback for a heat pump, rooftop solar PV, and loft and cavity wall insulation.

Status: prototype. Several inputs are placeholders (labelled orange on the page) and carbon results are switched off until the 2026 DESNZ grid electricity and gas factors are entered. Do not use the results for decisions until those are sourced.

Files: `index.html` (single self-contained page, no external libraries).

Sources and open items are in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.
