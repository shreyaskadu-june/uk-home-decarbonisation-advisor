Uncertainties and method
  • Heat pump running cost = electricity used (heat demand ÷ SCOP) × electricity price, minus the gas it replaces (gas use × boiler efficiency assumed 88%, from the gas bill). Standing charges are not included in the comparison.
  • Solar saving = self-used generation × electricity price + exported generation × export tariff. No battery is modelled.
  • Insulation savings are at 2024 prices and scaled to current gas prices, so they carry the 2024 house assumptions (three-bedroom semi, gas boiler).
  • Payback ignores price changes, maintenance and boiler replacement timing, and assumes the grant applies.
